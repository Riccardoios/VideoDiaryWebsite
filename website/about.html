About Video Diary

Made by Riccardo Carlotto · Updated November 2026

Video Diary is a video journaling app for iPhone and iPad, made by one person: Riccardo Carlotto, an independent iOS developer. Riccardo designs, builds and supports the app, and writes the guides on this site.

Who makes Video Diary?

Riccardo Carlotto makes Video Diary, and built it for a personal need first. In Riccardo's words: "I wanted a way to vent my emotions that I could re-watch later. Watching myself back, I see from a third-person perspective what I feel, who I am, what I like and what I don't. It lets me dive deep into my emotions and my patterns."

The first version of Video Diary went live on the App Store on 22 January 2022, and Riccardo has shipped updates ever since. Version 2.28 is the current release. The App Store lists Riccardo as the developer, and all of Riccardo's apps are on the App Store developer page.

Why does Video Diary have no AI?

Video Diary has no AI because a diary should be a place where nobody, and nothing, is reading along. The app records your video, keeps it on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you turn on iCloud backup), and leaves it there. No chatbot summarises your entries, no model analyses your face or your mood, and there is no feed to share to. The app does one job: you open it, tap record, and talk.

How are the guides on this site written?

Riccardo writes every guide from experience building a video journaling app, and checks competitor details against each company's own pricing pages, help documentation and App Store listing. Each guide shows when it was last updated. If you spot something out of date, send an email and it gets fixed.

Email Riccardo at developer.riccardo.mobile@gmail.com. That address covers bug reports, feature requests, questions about Premium, and press. Riccardo reads every message personally, so the reply comes from a person, not a bot.
